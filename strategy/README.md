# Ashwa Strategy: Thesis, Offers & Bet Lab

This folder holds the **business thesis** for Ashwa, rebuilt from first principles as a **bootstrapped pilot bet**: little to no capex, minimal opex, downside capped, upside uncapped.

`Ashwa_genesis.md` stays the canonical product-intent file. Once conjectures here are proven in the field, promote them into genesis. Until then, everything here is a **conjecture**.

## Operating rules (summary)

- Every initiative is a **bet card** (`08` P1). No card, no work.
- Linear costs, geometric returns. Long volatility.
- One school cluster until >40% driver penetration (Zepto-style density).
- Never: monetise child data, promise safety, lend from our own book, quit the day job before C1–C3 pass.
