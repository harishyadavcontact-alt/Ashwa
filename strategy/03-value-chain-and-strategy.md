# 03 — Value Chain, Working Backwards & Strategy

Status: v0.1. Frameworks: value-chain decoupling (Thales Teixeira), working backwards (Amazon PR/FAQ), Peter Thiel (Zero to One), the Zepto playbook, and Spitznagel's roundabout investing (the "Umweg").

## 1. Customer value chain — where value leaks today (decoupling)

Decoupling: don't attack the whole chain. Pick the one link where the customer is worst served, own it, then expand to neighbouring links.

| CVC stage | Parent today | Driver today | Who serves it today | Decouple? | Ashwa move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Ask neighbours, WhatsApp groups, school gate | Word of mouth | Nobody (informal) | Later (L2) | Per-school pages, waitlist |
| **Evaluate / trust** | Gut feel, other parents | Reputation | Nobody | Later (L0) | Verification + vouches |
| **Agree / pay** | Cash, 2-month advance, summer fees | Chasing cash | Cash | Phase 1 (L3) | UPI collect + receipts |
| **Consume (daily ride)** | **Anxiety, calls, no visibility** | **Calls while driving** | **Nobody** | **YES: wedge** | **Live tracking + 6 alerts** |
| **Disruption** (sick day, quit) | Stranded child, scramble | Lost income, favours | Favours | Phase 1 (L4) | Backup pool |
| **Exit / switch** | Painful, start over | Empty seat for months | Nobody | Phase 1 (L2) | Vacancy fill + new matches |

**Why the "consume" link is the wedge:** it happens **twice a day, every school day**, so value is felt within 24 hours. It needs **no change in the commercial relationship** (no payment switch, no new driver). And it **makes the existing driver the distributor**. Once we own the daily moment, every other link is one feature away.

## 2. Working backwards — the press release we want to be true

> **Bangalore, June 2028.** *"No kid left waiting, no seat riding empty."* Ashwa now runs the daily school commute for 120,000 children across Bangalore, Chennai and Hyderabad. 9,000 independent van drivers use it, and 600 of them have grown into fleet owners with 5+ vans, financed through Ashwa's lending partners. Parents get six calm alerts a day. Drivers report zero "where are you" calls and fees collected on time. When a driver is sick, a verified backup is assigned within minutes. "My father ran vans for twenty years on memory and cash," says founder Harish. "Now an 18-year-old can go from one van to six in three years, on one app."

**FAQ (the hard ones):**

| Question | Answer we must be able to give |
|---|---|
| Why didn't Uber or Rapido do this? | Their model is on-demand strangers. This is a recurring trust relationship. Supply joins through a peer (driver), not an ad |
| How do you make money if tracking is free? | Paid-by-the-desperate first (match and fill fees), then fleet SaaS, then finance distribution |
| What stops drivers leaving with their customers? | Nothing, and that's the point. We make the driver's business better, so leaving costs them alerts, backups and payments |
| What was the hardest part? | Density, one school cluster at a time, and earning driver trust |

Working back from that: 2028 needs Phase 2 proof in 2027, which needs 3 dense clusters in early 2027, which needs **Dad's cluster at >40% driver penetration by the June 2027 term start**. That is the only date that matters right now. (Adjust to your real timeline.)

## 3. Vertical integration ladder — driver → owner → ecosystem

| Rung | Who | What Ashwa gives | What we earn | Trigger to the next rung |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Backup / relief driver | Shifts from the backup pool | % of shift | Learns routes, earns reputation |
| 1 | Single-van owner-driver | Tracking, invites, collections | Fill fees on new kids | Full van + waitlist overflow |
| 2 | 2-van owner | Hire a driver from the pool, fleet view | Per-van SaaS | Waitlist > 1 van of demand |
| 3 | 5–6-van fleet owner | Loan via partner, demand routing, driver attendance | SaaS + loan distribution fee | Off-peak demand available |
| 4 | Fleet + off-peak | Office/factory recurring routes | Ride margin | — |

**Key insight:** the waitlist *is* the credit signal. A driver with a full van, documented collections and a waitlist is a better borrower than a dealer's walk-in customer. That's how we get lenders to underwrite van #2 without lending from our own book (capex stays at 0).

## 4. Zepto vs Amazon → Ashwa vs Uber/Rapido

| Zepto principle | How Zepto used it | Ashwa analogue |
|---|---|---|
| **Density beats coverage** | Dark stores in tight radii; dominate one pin code before the next | Dominate **one school cluster** (≈ 1 km around a set of schools) before touching another |
| **One obsessive promise** | "10 minutes" | "Zero-call school run" for drivers; "Van in 48 hours" for new parents |
| **Own the hard last mile** | Built their own store ops; didn't depend on sellers | Own the driver relationship and onboarding in person (founder + Dad) |
| **Speed of execution** | Weekly launches, ops-first | Weekly field releases; ship to Dad on Monday, read the data on Friday |
| **Incumbent is structurally slow** | Amazon optimised for selection + 1–2 day delivery | Uber optimised for on-demand strangers; recurring school trust isn't their game |

**Optionality: acquired vs independent.** Both outcomes need the same asset: a **dense, trusted, recurring supply network plus its data**. Build that and both doors stay open. Don't build anything only an acquirer would value (e.g. a generic ride-hailing clone).

## 5. Peter Thiel — the seven questions

| Question | Our answer today | Confidence | What would raise it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engineering:** 10× better? | Calls → zero; a stranded child → backup in minutes | Medium | Dad's call count before/after |
| **Timing:** why now? | Cheap smartphones + UPI + WhatsApp everywhere; drivers already on WhatsApp | High | — |
| **Monopoly:** big share of a small market? | Dad's cluster: dozens of drivers, hundreds of kids. Can we get >40%? | Unknown | Pilot penetration |
| **People:** right team? | Insider founder; needs an ops/tech cofounder | Medium | Cofounder |
| **Distribution:** how do we sell? | Driver-led invites (supply brings demand) | Medium | C1/C2 results |
| **Durability:** defensible in 10 years? | Trust graph + density + regulator registry | Low–Medium | Retention after 6 months |
| **Secret:** what do we know others don't? | Trust flows *driver → parent*, not app → parent. Drivers fear exposure more than they want efficiency. The real economics (advances, summer fees, grey zones) never show up in surveys | High | — |

**Start small, monopolise, then expand. Last-mover advantage** comes from owning the trust graph per cluster.

**Reconciling Thiel and Spitznagel/Taleb:** be *definite* about the destination (the press release) and *non-predictive* about the path (many small, capped bets in `05`). We don't forecast which channel or feature wins. We set up cheap trials and let the market pick.

## 6. Roundabout investing (Umweg) — the indirect path

Take a small immediate loss for a large positional gain later.

| Direct (tempting) path | Roundabout path we take | Positional advantage gained |
|---|---|---|
| Charge drivers a commission now | 0% on existing customers, for life | Driver trust → driver-led distribution |
| Build a marketplace first | Build the daily tracking utility first | Daily habit; supply already on board when the marketplace switches on |
| Raise money now | Bootstrap to proof | Higher valuation, founder control |
| Expand across the city fast | Saturate one cluster | Density → fastest matches → hard to dislodge |
| Monetise child data | Never | Parent trust + regulatory goodwill |

## 7. Ownership — "Zuckerberg-type" founder control

| Goal | How (to verify with a lawyer) |
|---|---|
| Keep control while raising | Bootstrap to proof → raise less at a higher price; keep the founder above 50% through seed |
| Voting control later | India allows shares with differential voting rights (DVR) under Companies Act conditions. Many startups instead use a parent entity abroad (Delaware/Singapore) for dual-class. Decide at the seed stage, not now |
| Cofounder fairness | Standard 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff; equity for the cofounder sized to contribution |
| Avoid early dilution traps | No equity for "advisors" without vesting and defined deliverables |
| Protect IP | Make sure the code isn't claimed by your employer (check your contract; build on personal devices and time) |
